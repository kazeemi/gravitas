Here it is:

Add a first-login welcome screen to Gravitas.

Show this screen only once — on first login, before the user reaches the dashboard. Once they dismiss it, never show it again. A "How it works" link in the nav or footer should let returning users access it if needed.

Design: Full-screen welcome state using the existing Gravitas design system. Midnight background, amber accents, Georgia for headings, Calibri for body text. Not a modal — a full page the user actively exits by clicking the CTA button.

Content — display exactly as follows:

Headline (Georgia, large, cream): Welcome to Gravitas.

Subheadline (Georgia, italic, amber): Your personal executive presence coach. Here is how it works.

Three horizontal cards in a row (equal width, Midnight panel style, amber top accent):

Card 1 — Choose a prompt Select a professional scenario or write your own. Gravitas detects the context automatically — board presentation, difficult conversation, impromptu — and calibrates the scoring standard.

Card 2 — Record Record your response in audio or video mode. Audio scores 11 dimensions. Video scores all 15, including physical presence. Sessions run 1 to 10 minutes.

Card 3 — Get your scores and coaching Receive scores across 15 dimensions of executive presence. Every dimension includes evidence-based coaching feedback and one concrete action to take before your next session.

Below the three cards, a smaller note in muted text (Calibri, 12pt, not amber — subtle): After two sessions, your Progress page unlocks. You will see how your scores are moving across every dimension — where you are improving and where to focus next.

Bottom of screen, smallest text, cream at low opacity: Built on McKinsey-level analytical rigour and Executive Coaching methodology. Your recording is deleted immediately after scoring. During beta, transcripts are accessible to the Gravitas team for scoring validation only.

CTA button (full amber, Georgia, centred): Let's begin →

Technical notes:





Use a flag in the user record (e.g. has_seen_welcome: true) set on CTA click. Check this flag on every login — if true, skip directly to dashboard.



The CTA navigates to the Record screen.



Do not show an X or close button. The only exit is the CTA. This ensures the user reads the privacy line before proceeding.



On mobile, stack the three cards vertically rather than horizontally.



No animations needed. Static screen is fine.
