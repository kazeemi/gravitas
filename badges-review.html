How triggers work (code logic)

All 6 badges live in a single SESSION_BADGES array in session-reveal.tsx. Each entry has a threshold (number) and label (string).

On the score slide, the app fetches all sessions, filters to processingStatus === "complete", sorts by createdAt ascending, and finds the index of the current session. That index + 1 = session number.

If sessionNumber exactly matches any badge threshold, the badge fades in after a 2.5-second delay (so the score animation finishes first). Only one badge can appear per session.

There is also a separate tier-up moment (e.g. "Moved up to Strong") that can appear on the same slide — it triggers at 2.2 s and is independent of badges. No badge appears on a progress page or history — only on the live score reveal.

Gaps & things to consider when redesigning

All badges look identical

The only visual difference is the number in the circle. A badge for session 50 looks the same as session 1.

The number feels generic

Showing "50" in a circle reads as a number, not an achievement. Premium apps use icons, emblems, or unique glyphs per tier.

Language is flat

"Badge unlocked" + "First Step" is functional but not emotionally resonant. No copy beneath to mark the moment.

No sense of rarity

Nothing signals that "Elite" is rare or significant. A user who reaches 50 sessions should feel it.

No persistence

Badges aren't stored or shown anywhere after the reveal moment — no profile, no history wall. Once you leave the screen, it's gone.

No score-based badges

All badges are volume (count) only. There are no achievement badges for hitting a score threshold (e.g. first time scoring 8+).
