Every request received at info@selfcraftpartners.com (or any other channel) invoking a GDPR right — access, rectification, erasure, portability, objection, restriction, or a complaint — should be logged here the day it's received. GDPR requires a response within one month (Article 12(3)); this log is what lets you actually track that deadline instead of relying on memory.

Most rights (access, rectification, erasure, portability) are already self-service in Account Settings and don't need a log entry unless the user contacts you directly instead of using the feature, or the self-service path fails. Log every request that arrives by email or any manual channel.
